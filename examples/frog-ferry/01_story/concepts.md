# Concepts: a tiny origami frog sails a paper boat across a puddle to bring a dandelion seed to a snail; a raindrop keeps sinking her boat

Emotional core of the idea: small effort against a small, relentless nuisance, and the joy of a gift that arrives anyway. The comedy is a repeated gag (the drop) that finally turns into help.
Runtime/medium: 40 s, 2D cel animation, no dialogue, ages 3-7. Sized as 3 characters (one a faceless raindrop), 1 location (a puddle with two shores), 4 scenes.

## C1 — The Drop Is Not Evil
- **Logline (≤35 words):** A tiny origami frog sails to deliver a dandelion seed to a snail, but a raindrop keeps sinking her boat until she shields the seed with its own fluff and the drop becomes the helper.
- **Hook:** the raindrop hits the exact same spot every time, faster each time.
- **Engine:** want = deliver the seed · obstacle = rain keeps sinking her · cannot be avoided because the only way across is the puddle.
- **Central question:** Will the seed reach the snail?
- **Ending direction:** warm; the last raindrop waters the planted seed (the nuisance becomes the helper).
- **Twist potential:** the drop's bounce off the fluff pushes the boat ashore (clue: ripples and bounce shown at first sinking).
- **Producibility:** 3 characters, 1 location, no text, no dialogue.

## C2 — The Race Against the Snail
- **Logline (≤35 words):** A frog races a snail to a dandelion seed across a puddle while a raindrop interferes; both end up sharing the boat.
- **Hook:** fast frog vs slow snail.
- **Engine:** want = win the race · obstacle = the drop and the snail's slow steadiness.
- **Central question:** Who gets there first?
- **Ending direction:** tie, friendship.
- **Twist potential:** the snail was always aboard.
- **Producibility:** snail on the boat means two characters in nearly every shot (worse consistency); contradicts "deliver a seed TO a snail".

## C3 — Boat in a Bottle Cap
- **Logline (≤35 words):** A frog folds ever-sillier boats to beat the rain; each folded boat sinks in a new way.
- **Hook:** repeated fold-and-sink gag.
- **Engine:** want = deliver seed · obstacle = new gag each time.
- **Central question:** Which boat survives?
- **Ending direction:** the simplest boat wins.
- **Twist potential:** none.
- **Producibility:** many boat variants multiply reference art; no emotional turn.
