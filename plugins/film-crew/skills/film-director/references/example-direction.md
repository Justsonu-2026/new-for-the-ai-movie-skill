# Example: One Directed Scene

Scene (from the screenwriter's example): INT. APARTMENT BUILDING - THIRD FLOOR LANDING - NIGHT. Maya
delivers to "4B"; Mr. Adebayo says there is no fourth floor; she reads her own address on the label.

## Analysis
- Maya wants to finish the delivery and get paid; obstacle: the address doesn't exist; tactic: insist, then check.
- Mr. Adebayo wants to protect his door, then to help; tactic: deny, then offer.
- Subtext: she has been sent to find herself. Turn: the label (certain → disoriented).
- Audience position: same as Maya (mystery).
- **Visual thesis:** the chain on the door stays between them; when she reads her name, he stops mattering —
  the frame loses him.

## Beats
| Beat | Change | Lines |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | arrival, knock | action 1-2 |
| B2 | denial: "There is no four." | dialogue 1-2 |
| B3 | proof: the painted 3 | action 3 |
| B4 | the label (turn) | insert + action |
| B5 | offer refused; she leaves | dialogue 3, action |

## Blocking
Floor plan LOC-LANDING: stairs up from south, door D2 on the east wall, blank wall with painted "3" to the north.
- Start: Maya at top step facing east toward D2. Mr. Adebayo inside D2 behind the chain.
- B3: Maya turns north to the painted 3 (motivated by his look). B5: she exits south, down the stairs.
- Axis: Maya–door line (west–east). Camera stays on the south side (stairs side) for B1-B4.
- Screen direction: Maya faces screen right toward the door and exits screen left down the stairs, so SC04
  must continue her movement screen left (noted for the script supervisor).

## Performance
- B2 Maya: to insist — weight forward, parcel held out. Adebayo: to refuse — doesn't look at the parcel,
  looks past her up the stairs.
- B4 Maya: to understand — stillness, thumb stops mid-wipe, breath held.
- B5 Adebayo: to reach out — voice drops, a half-step toward the chain. Maya: to escape — moves before he finishes.
