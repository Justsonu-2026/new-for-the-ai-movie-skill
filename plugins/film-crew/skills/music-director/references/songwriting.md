## 1. Why this song is in the film
A song must do story work: express a character's want (the "I want" song in musicals), mark a change,
carry a montage, set a world, or comment ironically. Write its purpose in one line, and what is different
in the story after it.

## 2. Structure
Common forms: verse-chorus (V-C-V-C-Bridge-C), verse-pre-chorus-chorus, AABA (32-bar standard), through-
composed (story songs). Verses move the story forward (new information each time); the chorus states the
central idea and returns; the bridge changes perspective. For film, fit the structure to the scene length:
a 60-second montage might be V-C-C.

## 3. Lyrics
- Concrete images over abstractions ("cold tea on the windowsill" vs "I'm sad").
- One idea per section; each verse adds new detail.
- The title (and hook) appears in the chorus, often first or last line.
- Consistent point of view and tense.
- Rhyme: perfect and near rhymes; choose a scheme per section (ABAB, AABB, XAXA) and keep it.
- Write in the character's voice (vocabulary from the voice profile).

## 4. Prosody
Prosody is the fit between words and music:
- Natural word stress lands on strong beats ("to-NIGHT", not "TO-night").
- Long notes on open vowels (ah, oh, ay); avoid long notes on closed sounds (ee in "sweet" is singable;
  "it", "with" on a long note are not).
- Line lengths roughly matched within a section so the melody can repeat.
- Emotional peaks of the lyric coincide with melodic peaks.

## 5. Hooks
A hook is the memorable fragment — a lyric phrase, a melodic figure or a rhythm. Place the lyric hook at the
start or end of the chorus and repeat it; a short instrumental hook can also mark the theme in the score.

## 6. Melody and arrangement notes
Give: tempo (BPM), meter, key or mode, vocal range (e.g. A3-D5) suited to the character's voice, melodic
contour per section (verse low and conversational, chorus higher), arrangement build (sparse verse, fuller
chorus), and genre/era instruments.

## 7. Lyric sheet format
```
SONG-RAIN — "Paper Boats" (sung by MAYA, SC04, ~70 s)
Purpose: Maya admits she wanted to be found. After the song she opens the parcel.
Form: V1 – C – V2 – C – Bridge – C (short)
Tempo 84 BPM, 4/4, D major (bridge to B minor), range A3–C#5

[Verse 1]
I fold the street maps into boats ...   (A)
...
[Chorus]
...
Prosody notes: "found" on the downbeat of bar 4, held 2 beats.
```
