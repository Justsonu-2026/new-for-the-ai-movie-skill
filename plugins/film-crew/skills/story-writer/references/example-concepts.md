# Worked Example: from idea to concepts to beats

Idea from the user: "a lonely old robot in a junkyard". Target: 90 s, general audience, 3D animation.

## Concepts (abridged)

**C1 — The Last Part.** A junkyard robot keeps repairing a broken music box to hear the song its owner used
to play; when the final gear is found inside its own chest, it must choose between the song and staying
powered.
- Hook: a machine willing to switch itself off for a memory. Central question: will it give up its gear?
- Twist potential: the song was never the owner's — the robot composed it. Producibility: 1 character, 1 location.

**C2 — Night Shift.** A robot scares off scavengers every night to protect "its" junkyard — until a
scavenger turns out to be a lost child who needs to get home before dawn.
- Hook: guardian becomes guide. Central question: will it leave the yard it has never left?
- Producibility: 2 characters, 2 locations; one chase.

**C3 — Rust Garden.** A robot secretly grows flowers in crushed cars; the yard's crusher is scheduled for
the morning. Kishōtenketsu shape: no villain, the twist is that the crusher operator has been watering them.
- Producibility: 2 characters, 1 location; gentle tone.

Recommendation: C1 — strongest single choice and fits 90 s with one character.

## Developed beats for C1 (five-beat short)

| Beat | What happens | Connector | Value | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 Setup | Robot sorts scrap at dusk, winds a broken music box; it plays two notes and stops | — | lonely, routine | 15 s |
| B2 Inciting | Robot finds the music box's missing comb but not the last gear | therefore | hope | 15 s |
| B3 Escalation | It dismantles the yard searching; its own chest panel rattles each time it bends | but | hope → desperation | 25 s |
| B4 Climax | It opens its chest: the gear is its own core. It pulls it, fits it, the song plays as its lights fade | therefore | desperation → peace | 25 s |
| B5 Resolution | Morning: a girl climbing the fence hears the music box, still playing beside the still robot | — | grief → wonder | 10 s |

Setups/payoffs: chest rattle (B3) → gear in chest (B4); two notes (B1) → full song (B4); fence (B1
background) → girl climbing it (B5).

Rubric: Hook 4, Stakes 5, Causality 5, Agency 5, Emotional turn 5, Twist n/a 5, Payoff 5, Originality 4,
Producibility 5.
