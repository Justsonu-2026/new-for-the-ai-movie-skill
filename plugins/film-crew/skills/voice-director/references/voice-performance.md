## 1. Voice descriptors
| Dimension | Examples |
|---|---|
| Age impression | child, teen, young adult, middle-aged, elderly |
| Pitch | high, mid, low; range narrow/wide |
| Timbre / texture | bright, warm, dark, breathy, husky, gravelly, nasal, clear, raspy |
| Resonance | chesty, head voice, thin, full |
| Pace | quick, measured, slow, uneven |
| Rhythm | clipped, flowing, halting, sing-song |
| Energy | restrained, lively, intense, tired |
| Accent / region | named respectfully; light or strong |
| Under stress | gets faster and higher / goes quiet and flat / cracks |
Profile example: "Teen girl, mid-low pitch, slightly husky, quick clipped rhythm, light East African
English accent; under stress she gets quieter and slower, not louder."

## 2. Delivery notation
Per line:
```
VL-SC03-004  MAYA (on screen, lip-sync)  window 3.0-3.8 s
"That's my street."
intention: to understand (to herself)
stress: "my"
pace: slow; volume: barely audible; pause before "street" (0.2 s)
breath: small intake before the line
transition: from silence after the label insert
```

## 3. Pace and timing
Conversational speech is roughly 150 words per minute (~2.5 words/s); tense arguments run faster, grief and
intimacy slower. Pauses carry meaning: before a hard word, after a revelation. Timing windows come from the
shot records; deliveries must fit them.

## 4. Emphasis and subtext
The operative word carries the intention. "*That's* my street" (disbelief), "That's *my* street"
(personal), "That's my *street*" (location). Pick based on the scene's subtext and the director's playable
action; mark it.

## 5. Non-verbal sounds
Breaths, sighs, laughs, gasps, swallowing, effort sounds in action scenes ("efforts"), crying. List them as
lines too when they need to be generated (they need timing and lip/face sync as well).

## 6. Take review checklist
- [ ] Words exact; no added or dropped words
- [ ] Pronunciation per guide
- [ ] Same voice identity as approved lines (timbre, accent, age)
- [ ] Delivery: intention, stress, pace, volume as directed
- [ ] Fits the timing window
- [ ] No artifacts: clicks, robotic prosody, cut breaths, metallic tone
- [ ] Level and room tone consistent with neighbouring lines (sound-designer finishes the match)
