# Example: Rewriting One Scene

## Source (user's draft)

> Maya gets to the apartment. She is tired and sad because her mom is sick and she needs money. She knocks
> and an old man answers. She says "I have a delivery for apartment 4B, it's very important, I came all the
> way across the city in the rain." He says "There's no 4B here, this building only has three floors." She
> is confused. She looks at the parcel and sees her own address. She realizes the parcel is for her.

## Diagnosis
- Interior states told, not shown ("tired and sad", "confused").
- Exposition in dialogue ("I came all the way across the city in the rain") — the audience saw the journey.
- The reveal (her own address) is rushed: no pause, no reaction.
- Backstory (sick mother) is stated in action, which can't be filmed.

## Scene card
- Purpose: the delivery cannot be completed; the twist is set up. Value: certain → disoriented.
- Who speaks: the old man mostly; Maya says little — her silence plays the shock.
- What happens: she knocks; he denies 4B exists; she checks the label; she sees her own address.
- What we see: the dripping parcel, the stairwell number "3" as the top floor, her face as she reads.
- Duration: ~25 s.

## Rewrite

```
INT. APARTMENT BUILDING - THIRD FLOOR LANDING - NIGHT

Rain hammers a skylight. MAYA (17), soaked to the shoulders, knocks on the last door. Water drips from
the parcel under her arm onto the floor.

The door opens on a chain. MR. ADEBAYO (70s), cardigan, reading glasses on a cord.

MAYA
Delivery. Four B.

MR. ADEBAYO
(looking past her, up the stairs)
There is no four.

Maya follows his look. The stairs end at a blank wall. A painted "3" on it.

She turns the parcel over. Wipes the label with her thumb.

INSERT - THE LABEL
Her own name. Her own street.

Mr. Adebayo watches her face. The chain stays on.

MR. ADEBAYO (CONT'D)
(gentler)
You want to come in out of the wet?

Maya doesn't answer. She is already moving down the stairs.
```

## Changes table
| Element | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "tired and sad" | changed → soaked, dripping parcel | filmable |
| sick mother line | cut here | backstory belongs in an earlier scene as an image |
| Maya's long line | cut to "Delivery. Four B." | journey already seen; brevity sells exhaustion |
| painted "3" on wall | added | visual proof there is no fourth floor |
| reaction beat after label | added | the twist needs time to land |
| Mr. Adebayo's offer | added | gives the old man a human beat; her refusal shows her state |
